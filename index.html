How We Help

No stress. No judgment. Just real help when you need it.

🔋 Jump Start
You wake up in the morning, ready to go to work — and the car just won't start. Don't panic. Call us and we'll come to you right away. We safely jump your battery and get you back on the road in minutes.
🚗 Recovery / Winch Out
Your car slid off the road or got stuck in a ditch? You’re stressed, you’re not sure what to do. Just call us. We recover the vehicle safely, pull it out without extra damage, and help you continue your trip.
🛠️ Tire Change
Flat tire on the highway? On your driveway? Middle of the night? We’ll arrive fast, replace the tire safely, and make sure you’re good to drive — no crawling on the shoulder, no risk.
🔑 Lockout Service
Keys locked inside your car? It happens to everybody. Call us and we’ll unlock the vehicle quickly and without damage, so you can get back in and keep moving.
⛽ Fuel Delivery
Ran out of gas on the way somewhere important? Stay where you are. We’ll bring fuel directly to you so you don’t have to walk, hitchhike, or leave the car alone.
🚚 Local & Long-Distance Towing
Need your vehicle moved safely? We tow cars, trucks, SUVs — locally or long-distance. We serve Bonney Lake, WA and up to 200 miles around the Seattle area. Ask for a quote by phone.
